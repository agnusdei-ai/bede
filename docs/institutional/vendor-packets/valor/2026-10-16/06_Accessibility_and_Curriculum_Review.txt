Instructional approach and intended scope
Bede is designed around question-led teaching, short guided lessons, narration and work with real texts. The intended
learning range is elementary and middle grades (K-8), with the specific classroom grade, language, subject and source
materials to be approved by the School before use. Public charter-school use must follow the School-approved
curriculum and applicable educational policies; any optional religious content must be excluded unless expressly
permitted in that setting.

Accessibility status: design intentions are not test results
Target relevant WCAG success criteria for the web/application experience and applicable school obligations. The U.S.
Department of Justice Title II web/mobile rule uses WCAG 2.1 Level AA for covered public entities, subject to its
exceptions, applicability and compliance dates; Bede may use WCAG 2.2 AA as an internal design target. Agnus Dei
has not supplied an independently reviewed VPAT/ACR or completed institutional assistive-technology audit in this
packet.

Evidence Valor may ask for
   Documented keyboard and screen-reader tests on the exact browser/device build, including enrollment, lesson,
    evidence review and denial dialogs.
   Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR/VPAT) if requested, with identified nonconformities and written
    remediation commitments.
   Representative lesson samples with curriculum map; source rights or school-owned content authorization.
   Educator review of suitability for actual grades, English learners and students with disabilities.

Classroom content and assessment boundaries
External assessments such as DIBELS 8 must be administered under the instrument owner’s terms by authorized
personnel. Bede must not manufacture scores or make unsupported growth claims. For standardized school subjects,
an approved teaching plan and materials inventory should be attached before real student participation.
