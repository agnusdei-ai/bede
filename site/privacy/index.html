Privacy & Data Disclosure

How the website and hosted demo use information, browser storage, and outside services. Last reviewed 2026-10-20.

Two Different Things Share This Domain

The marketing site, and the demo, are not the same product

The public pages introduce Bede. You can send a business inquiry at /inquire/, share feedback at /survey/, or use an email link. The interactive demo under /bede/ is a different thing entirely: a separate, time-limited, fully-featured free trial that runs in the cloud, on our own servers, so you can try Bede without installing anything. It is not the product a family actually buys.

Once purchased, Bede runs entirely on hardware a family owns, in their own home. No lesson text, no voice recording, and no biometric profile leaves that house unless the family deliberately configures a cloud AI provider themselves. The table below is split accordingly: the marketing site first, then the demo. Website forms and analytics also handle information, as described below.

The marketing site (this domain, outside /bede/)

Styles, fonts, motion, and illustrations use files served by this website. A Cloudflare measurement script is included in the page HTML, as described below. Inquiry and survey answers are not saved in browser storage.

Forms send information only when you choose to submit them. Email links open your email application; you decide whether to send. Please do not include student records or sensitive information. No payment details are collected on these pages.

The interactive demo (/bede/)

The demo also includes the Cloudflare script described above, whose load is currently blocked. Its separate data-use consent step covers tutoring features; it does not control that beacon. Launch Bede demo →. The browser storage and service inventory follows.
