Co-op and educator FAQs

Can our co-op share a household membership?

Household memberships cover one family. Co-ops, cohorts, and independent educators arrange a separate offer based on their setting.

How does Bede fit between meetings?

Families use guided questions, narration, and reading alongside their chosen curriculum. Adults set goals and review the work between co-op sessions.

What details help you plan our setup?

Estimated families, learners, educators, locations, devices, and timing. Include curriculum or integration needs; estimates are fine.
