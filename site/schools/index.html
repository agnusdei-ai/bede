What a campus licence includes

  • Unlimited enrolled learner seats at one licensed campus location.
  • Educator accounts and a roster for the campus.
  • Educator-supplied assessment records, kept separate from Bede's own diagnostics.

What a campus licence does not include

A campus licence does not make these unlimited:

  • AI inference throughput.
  • Simultaneous user capacity.
  • Uptime, or hardware suitability for your building.

These are not included unless your agreement names them: Mac mini hardware (sold separately), custom student-information-system or learning-management integrations, bespoke curriculum, onsite services, and custom infrastructure. Each additional physical location is licensed separately.

How educators stay in control

The school sets the plan. Bede defers to the teacher as the primary educator, follows the school's curriculum, and records work as factual observations rather than grades of character or faith.

Frequently asked questions

What is Bede?

Bede is a Socratic AI tutor for classical homeschool and school programs. It asks questions rather than giving answers, runs short daily subject sessions, and works alongside the real books and the teacher's own plan. It is made by Agnus Dei Technologies, LLC.

What does a campus licence cover?

A campus licence covers unlimited enrolled learner seats at one licensed campus location. Each additional physical location is licensed separately.

What does a campus licence not cover?

A campus licence does not make AI inference throughput, simultaneous user capacity, uptime, or hardware suitability unlimited. Mac mini hardware is sold separately and is not included unless it is named in your agreement. Custom student-information-system or learning-management integrations, bespoke curriculum, onsite services and custom infrastructure are scoped separately.

Does Bede administer DIBELS assessments?

No. DIBELS is a trademark of the University of Oregon. Bede does not administer, score, or import DIBELS assessments and does not use outside results in its own diagnostics. A trained educator uses the official materials independently and may record the result in Bede as the educator reports it.

Does Bede grade students on character or faith?

No. Classical formative observations are factual records of work, not grades of character, spirituality, curiosity, virtue or aptitude. Faith formation remains the family's or school's own responsibility, and Bede does not evaluate a child's spiritual life.

Who controls what is taught?

The educator or the family sets the plan. Bede is designed to defer to the teacher as the primary educator and to follow the curriculum the school already uses.

Where is student data stored?

Student data in a self-hosted deployment is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and stays on the school's own equipment. Read the full data disclosure before deciding.
