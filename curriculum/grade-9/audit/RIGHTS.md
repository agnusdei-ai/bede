# Source-and-rights review guide

The [JSON register](source-register.json) is a research artifact, **not an ingestion manifest**. It assesses copying, distribution, adaptation, TTS, storage, indexing and model processing separately. All records prohibit model training by default. `candidate` is not approved; `external-only` limits scope to cited consultation; `HOLD` marks unresolved blockers; `reject` excludes the proposed use.

Each entry records ID, identity/version, publisher/URL, intended use, exact known license or explicit uncertainty, evidence/date, territory, exceptions, attribution, use judgments, access/demand/currency/context, equipment/cost, reviewer, disposition and reason. Where primary evidence remains incomplete, missing imprint/asset ownership is explicit rather than invented. Science references are separate exact URL records with their full existing citation/selection metadata, not blanket government-site permission.

- **USG/UNG history:** HOLD for operational adoption until normal secure access and accessible selection are confirmed. Original prose is CC BY-SA 4.0; images, embedded quotations and other separately licensed inserts are excluded. ShareAlike may apply to adapted source material, not automatically every independent Bede question or software file. Do not embed licensed prose and then label it proprietary.

- **OpenStax:** reject for this proposed commercial/AI corpus; owner chose replacement, not publisher outreach. Current Biology preface freshly confirms NC-SA. Prior records identify the other current prefaces. No historic CC BY copy has been authenticated; irrevocability of an actual older grant must not be confused with proof that this project possesses one.
- **Original biology notes:** candidate proprietary material with existing scoped review, not a certified lab course. References support independently expressed facts; government hosting does not clear all artwork, contributions or provider processing.
- **Adult science articles:** exact article license findings are preserved; clinical methods, figures/supplements and derivative assignments are excluded. Article accessibility and scientific claims must be reviewed for their intended level; they are not assigned as learner textbook chapters.

## Necessary gates

Before any asset is copied into delivery, obtain exact bytes/version and source notice, territory/use-specific review, attribution/change notices, excluded-material inventory, accessible-format check and authorized version-bound approval. TTS entails text rights, recording/voice/provider terms and learner privacy; permission to read does not settle all three. Storage, retrieval/indexing, model prompting and training are distinct operations.

The current Bede verbatim gate permits verified public domain, **not all commercially licensed material**. Do not mark CC BY or BY-SA assets as public domain to pass it. A future licensed-scope contract requires separately reviewed implementation; this task changes no runtime gate.

No statutory education/fair-use exception is asserted as a blanket permission for paid redistribution or AI processing. If legal uncertainty matters to an expanded use, HOLD or replace that asset. No purchases, permissions correspondence, bulk download, training or corpus activation occurred.
