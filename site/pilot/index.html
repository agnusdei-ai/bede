Bede / Guided Pilot

Classical learning, guided by educators.

Apply to join Bede's four-week, supervised pilot. Bede helps teachers and homeschooling parents plan instruction, review learning evidence, and approve each follow-up. Applications are reviewed before invitations are issued.

School or executive sponsor: apply → Homeschool family: apply →

These links open our existing secure business inquiry form. Submitting a request does not create a learner account, grant software access, or authorize student-data collection.

1. Discovery

Tell us about your organization or family, grade bands, estimated learner and educator counts, deployment needs, and intended timeline. Do not submit student names or records.

2. Owner review

Agnus Dei reviews each request and contacts eligible sponsors. Pilot enrollment and access remain invitation-only and manually approved.

3. Data qualification

Before any real student records are introduced, the authorized school administrator or executive must approve participating cohorts, observation roles, permitted data uses, and the end-of-pilot deletion plan.

4. Pilot and feedback

Approved participants complete supervised lessons. After the four-week pilot, educators and parents provide feedback through a separate privacy-protected survey.

What happens to pilot data?

Student data is not requested through this website form. The institution must authorize any later use of real learner records. If the pilot ends without a paid license, access is revoked and a verified data-deletion process must address retained records and backups; uninstall alone is not proof of deletion.

Feedback may inform Agnus Dei's product and instructional evaluation processes using privacy-preserving analysis. Pilot responses are not used to train or fine-tune AI models.
