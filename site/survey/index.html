The beta survey

About five minutes. It asks three things: whether the teaching is actually working for your children, what Bede did to your own day, and what a year of this should cost. Nothing here asks you to rate your child. Skip anything you would rather not answer, and leave the last two boxes blank to stay anonymous.

If what you have is a bug or something confusing, the feedback form is the better place for it and we read those first.

How many school days has your child actually used Bede? Everything below reads differently depending on this answer, so it is the one question worth being exact about.
The most useful answer on this page comes from a family it did not work for. Please be blunt.
Which grade bands are you teaching?
How many of your children are using it?
What happened to your own teaching time? The honest promise here is hours, not enthusiasm. If Bede costs you time, that outweighs everything else on this page.
When your child narrates to Bede, what Bede does with it is: Measured against you, because you are the real alternative. A tutor that is merely good is not obviously worth having.
Has Bede ever said something you had to correct? One credible report here matters more to us than every other answer on this page combined.
Does your child go back to it willingly?
How is the Socratic questioning landing? Bede asks rather than tells, and backs off to a hint after a round or two. We would rather hear that it frustrates your child than not hear it.
Which one subject is doing the most work for you?
Which would you turn off tomorrow?
Does the Progress page tell you anything you did not already know? "Have not opened it" is a real answer and we would rather know.
Do the faith modules fit your family? Scripture & Bible Study and Saints & Catechism are separate subjects on purpose. Tell us if that split gets your tradition wrong.
What do you spend in a year on the things Bede would replace or reduce? Curriculum, tutoring, subscriptions, workbooks. A rough figure is fine. We ask this before asking about price, on purpose.
Whatever you name here is our real competition, and it is probably not another AI.
Four short questions about price, and then we are nearly done. Rough numbers are fine.
Which shape fits your family best?
Would you rather run it yourself or have us host it? Bede is built to run on your own computer, which is why your children's data never reaches us. That is only worth something if you actually want it.
Optional, and it genuinely helps. We would rather be introduced than turn up cold.
Optional. Leave both blank and this stays anonymous.
Would you talk to us for twenty minutes?

This goes straight to us and nowhere else: no form service, no analytics, no tracker. Your answers are emailed to our own inbox and not stored on a server. If we cannot be reached, your own email program opens with the answers filled in instead, so nothing you typed is lost. Either way you can write to info@agnusdei.ai directly.
