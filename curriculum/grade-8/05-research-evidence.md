# Primary-source educational evidence register

**Research review assembled November 3, 2026.** Findings below concern other interventions/populations, not Bede. Verification level is stated; no supplied attachment's claims were accepted without checking a primary publisher, study author text or commissioning body's report page. Study summaries are not corpus permissions.

## E01 — Explicit secondary writing

**Source:** Graham et al., *Teaching Secondary Students to Write Effectively*, IES/WWC practice guide,2016, accessible revision2019. [Official guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Docs/PracticeGuide/508_WWCPG_SecondaryWriting_122719.pdf).

**Class/population:** evidence-based guidance synthesizing randomized/quasi-experimental studies for grades6–12; not itself a new Bede trial. **Verified:** primary guide's recommendations/evidence discussion inspected. Recommendation1 explicitly teaches strategies using **Model–Practice–Reflect**, rated strong evidence. The guide also integrates reading/writing and uses assessment to inform instruction/feedback; recommendation evidence levels differ.

**Decision relevance:** reviewed writing packets need explicit strategy demonstration, guided practice, independent attempt, reflection and revision. Narration/seminar or “write an essay” alone does not implement the recommendation. **Limits:** guide does not endorse a particular curriculum, software or classical model, nor establish Bede effects.

## E02 — Off-the-shelf lesson materials: final published trial

**Source:** C. Kirabo Jackson and Alexey Makarin,2018, *American Economic Journal: Economic Policy*10(3):226–254, [final publication/abstract](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/pol.20170211).

**Class/population:** randomized field experiment providing middle-school mathematics teachers high-quality existing online lessons, with/without use support. **Verified:** final journal publisher abstract, not a full reanalysis of data/appendices.

**Measured result:** access alone increased math achievement by a **marginally significant0.06SD**; access plus support by **0.09SD**, as the final abstract reports. Earlier working-paper estimates are not substituted. **Relevance:** reviewed reusable materials/support may matter independently of orchestration.

**Limits:** no generative Bede system, no all-subject Grade8–12 result, no guaranteed savings or universal teacher effect. Do not label an orchestration package's benefit as software-only when materials/support changed simultaneously.

## E03 — Homework, Organization and Planning Skills

**Source:** Langberg, Epstein, Becker, Girio-Herrera and Vaughn,2012, [author manuscript of published trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4209597/).

**Class/population:** randomized waitlist comparison,47 students with ADHD in grades6–8, delivered by17 school mental-health providers from five districts. **Verified:** primary study abstract/method/result passages, including parent-versus-teacher rating distinction.

**Measured outcomes:** parent-rated organized action/material management/planning/homework behaviors improved; teacher ratings did **not** show significant group improvement. The reported parent-rating effect sizes are not transferable effect sizes for a reminder app. **Relevance:** organization coaching should teach routines, materials management, assignment tracking and planning with monitored goals, not just notifications.

**Limits:** small clinical/school sample, waitlist comparison, rater differences and implementation context; no claim of cure or all-learner results. HOPS manual, forms and instruments are not licensed by free article access. Qualified intervention/referral needs remain.

## E04 — Adolescent literacy

**Source:** Kamil et al.,2008, IES, *Improving Adolescent Literacy: Effective Classroom and Intervention Practices*. [Official guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/docs/practiceguide/adlit_pg_082608.pdf).

**Class/population:** guidance for upper-elementary, middle and high school based on multiple studies; not a single randomized intervention. **Verified:** recommendations/publication framing in primary guide.

**Relevance:** explicit vocabulary and comprehension strategies, discussion of meaning, motivation/engagement and intensive individualized intervention by qualified specialists when needed. Separate access accommodations from instruction in reading skills.

**Limits:** age/study/intervention heterogeneity; choice of substantial texts does not by itself supply decoding intervention or validate a classical model. Do not replace indicated specialist instruction with TTS alone.

## E05 — Text-to-speech/read-aloud support

**Source:** Wood, Moxley, Tighe and Wagner, published online2017/final volume2018, *Journal of Learning Disabilities*51(1):73–84. [Author manuscript/meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5494021/).

**Class/population:** meta-analysis of heterogeneous studies of students with reading difficulties/disabilities, not solely Grade8. **Verified:** primary review abstract/method passages. Average reading-comprehension effect reported **d=.35,95%CI .14–.56**, with study-design moderation.

**Relevance:** individually trial lawful TTS/read-aloud as access support and check comprehension; retain supports during comparison conditions. **Limits:** not proof of improved decoding, automatic benefit to everyone, cure or Bede outcome. Mixed designs/context and older tools limit simple generalization. Analyze comprehension separately from decoding fluency.

## E06 — Speech-to-text for adolescents

**Source:** Matre et al., scoping review, online2022/final2024, [primary PubMed record/abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36427182/), DOI10.1080/17483107.2022.2149865.

**Class/population:** scoping review of STT for adolescents with learning difficulties in secondary education. **Verified:** abstract only; full study quality tables not inspected here.

**Relevance:** potentially useful writing access support, with instruction in dictation, punctuation, inspection and revision; score composition separately from transcription. **Limits:** abstract explicitly notes a paucity of high-quality research. No pooled causal estimate or cure is asserted; recognition errors and implementation matter. Full-text review is required before stronger claims.

## E07 — ChatGPT lesson-preparation time

**Source:** EEF *ChatGPT in lesson preparation — Teacher Choices trial*, [commissioner's primary project/results page](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/projects/choices-in-edtech-using-generative-ai-chatgpt-for-ks3-science-lesson-preparation-2024-teacher-choices-trial).

**Class/population:** randomized Teacher Choices evaluation,259 teachers/68 English schools, science teachers teaching Year7/8, with a guide supporting ChatGPT use. **Verified:** commissioner's reported design/results; full evaluation appendix not independently reanalyzed.

**Measured outcomes:** selected lesson/resource preparation time **56.2 vs81.5minutes/week**, reported difference25.3minutes/31%; blinded expert review did not detect an apparent quality reduction in reviewed resources.

**Relevance:** measure a bounded repetitive task and expert quality checks. **Limits:** not total workload, burnout, pupil outcomes, all subjects, parents, Bede or comprehensive net burden. “No apparent quality reduction” on these resources is not a noninferiority proof of learning preservation.

## E08 — Dialogic teaching

**Source:** EEF [Dialogic Teaching primary project/report page](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/projects/dialogic-teaching).

**Class/population:** randomized school evaluation centered on **Year5 primary pupils**, trained teachers, mentoring/video reflection and structured classroom talk. **Verified:** commissioning body's design/results summary, not full replication.

**Measured outcomes:** page reports positive English/science/maths effects summarized as around two months' progress. **Relevance:** discussion quality, teacher development and evidence-based reasoning matter.

**Limits:** younger population, intervention/support package, different curriculum and context. Do not export the months-progress figure to Grade8–12, Bede or an ordinary untrained seminar.

## E09 — Historical inquiry

**Class:** design requirement supported here by disciplinary practice and the inspected history-resource approach, **not a newly verified causal study**. Teach context, sourcing, corroboration and interpretation explicitly using exact, appropriately licensed sources and model the reasoning before independent analysis.

**Limit:** a generic source-analysis worksheet does not prove historical understanding or teacher relief. No empirical effect size is claimed; a future outcome map needs independent/new-source tasks and human scoring.

## What the evidence does not establish

No classical superiority, Bede learning acceleration, net time saving, reduced burnout, accurate automated placement or disability cure. No validation of a full Grade8 year or the proposed eight-week duration. Primary-source verification remains bounded: where only an abstract/commissioner summary was inspected, full appendices/data have not been evaluated. These are design inputs with explicit limits, not a marketing evidence bundle.
