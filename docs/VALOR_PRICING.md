At **104** licensed learners, 104 × $25 = **$2,600/month**, equal to the founding-campus rate; beyond 104, the founding-campus rate is less expensive if eligible. The regular per-learner offer is approximately 25–100 learners, so 101–103 learners require an individually agreed selection rather than an unadvertised automatic tier. The 25-seat floor implies a $625/month minimum or $7,500 per 12-month term. The founding-campus 12-month commitment totals **$31,200**, compared with **$42,000** for 12 months of the general $3,500/month campus price.

The **$2,600/month** founding-school membership is an approved promotional offer for eligible campus agreements entered during Fall 2026 through Spring 2027. Billing is monthly under a signed 12-month commitment. The agreed price applies throughout each signed 12-month subscription term, even when that term extends beyond Spring 2027. Eligibility after the promotional enrollment window and subsequent renewal rates require their own approved offer terms; the founding price is not automatically indefinite. 

All prices are USD; campus-specific taxes, hardware, and separately scoped professional services are excluded unless explicitly agreed. Do not present pilot pricing as a recurring monthly charge. Published pricing is for budgeting and inquiry, not a signed contract or a promise that institutional production deployment is already qualified. Each additional physical campus needs a separate license; hardware and separately scoped services are extra. The current invite-only Valor pilot stays free, manually approved and synthetic-data-only, without self-service purchasing, Stripe, or automatic paid conversion.

## Flexible licensing within a 12-month term

Schools may switch between per-learner and per-campus software pricing by **mutually agreed written contract amendment**, defining learner/campus scope, effective date, and new monthly rate. The 12-month contractual commitment remains in force unless expressly amended. Changes apply from the agreed effective date and must not double-bill the same campus period. Any proration, credits, or reapplication of promotional pricing requires express agreement; none is automatic.

## Included product scope

- Student Socratic learning companion with authorized instructional content (broader institutional offer only; not available in the current teacher-only Valor pilot).
- Teacher AI workspace: lesson support, learner pods, instructional review, and educator-approved interventions.
- Progress reporting: learner evidence, classroom summaries, campus insights, and permitted parent reporting; preserve provenance and human judgment.
- Educator identity/role boundaries, campus-scoped authorization, and privacy controls.
- Limited standard remote onboarding, setup guidance, initial roster configuration, standard reports, and product support. Define support SLAs before contracting.

No separate recurring fee for standard teacher tools, progress reporting, educator access controls, or the limited onboarding above. Custom SIS/LMS connectors, bespoke curriculum work, onsite services, unlimited coaching, and custom infrastructure changes are excluded and require separate scope/approval.

## Appliance / deployment assumptions

Apple Silicon Mac mini hardware is sold separately from software licensing. Proposed baselines: 32 GB unified memory / 1 TB storage for smaller deployments; 64 GB / 4 TB for larger campuses. These configurations are **not** approved concurrency or capacity guarantees. Qualify sustained student/educator concurrency, model throughput, response latency, backup/recovery, update procedure, and security before committing campus capacity.

## Pilot acceptance / contract guardrails

Before broader institutional release, verify teacher assignment-scoped access, student privacy and tenant separation, approved curriculum grounding, teacher override, evidence-backed reporting calculations, local inference/data-flow restrictions, backup/restore, security tests, and representative appliance load testing.

Do not imply official DIBELS 8 measures from Bede conversational data; integrate only authorized, provenance-preserving assessment records where relevant. Comprehension may be assessed through academic evidence; compassion and conscience should not be converted into AI-generated moral/character scores.
