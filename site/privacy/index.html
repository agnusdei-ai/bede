Privacy & Data Disclosure

A plain accounting of what this domain actually does: every cookie, every piece of browser storage, and every outside service contacted, with no rounding up. Last reviewed 2026-08-05.

Two Different Things Share This Domain

The marketing site, and the demo, are not the same product

The pages you are reading now (the home page, this page, the FAQ, the feedback form, and the two survey pages for parents and co-op leaders) are static. They contact nothing at all while you read them. The single exception is the moment you press send on one of the forms, and it goes to our own server and nowhere else, described below. The interactive demo at agnusdei.ai/bede/ is a different thing entirely: a separate, time-limited, fully-featured free trial that runs in the cloud, on our own servers, so you can try Bede without installing anything. It is not the product a family actually buys.

Once purchased, Bede runs entirely on hardware a family owns, in their own home. No lesson text, no voice recording, and no biometric profile leaves that house unless the family deliberately configures a cloud AI provider themselves. The table below is split accordingly: the marketing site first, then the demo, since only the demo collects anything at all.

The marketing site (this domain, outside /bede/)

No cookie is set anywhere on these pages. No browser storage of any kind (localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB) is used. No font, script, analytics tag, tag manager, or chat widget is loaded from any outside origin. The one exception is a single embedded video on the home page, and it is click-to-play: no request reaches YouTube until a visitor presses play themselves.

Two things do leave your browser, and only when you choose to make them. Pressing play on that video contacts YouTube. Pressing send on the feedback form or either survey posts what you typed to our own API, which emails it to us and stores nothing. Both are listed below. Until you do one of those, these pages make no network request of any kind.

ItemCategoryNotes
YouTube video embed (home page) Marketing / embedded video Loaded only after a visitor clicks “Watch: Meet Bede.” Served via YouTube's privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. Nothing is requested from YouTube before that click.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, and we never have. We do not use anything on this domain for advertising. We do not run analytics, an ad pixel, or a tracking script of any kind.
