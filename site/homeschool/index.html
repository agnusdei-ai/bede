THE BEDE PROMISE, IN PRACTICE

Learn deeply. Use what you learn.

Across the K–8 years, learning takes shape through close attention, clear expression, thoughtful judgment, and useful work.

  • 01Read closely. Notice meaning, sequence, and evidence in a text.
  • 02Write clearly. Put an observation, explanation, or argument into the learner’s own words.
  • 03Think through new scenarios. Bring ideas together, ask what follows, and explain a choice.
  • 04Apply knowledge in everyday work. Carry a lesson into a practical task, conversation, or decision.

GUIDED BY AN ADULT

Parents and educators choose the reading and practice, review the learner’s practical work, and discuss what it shows.

READ. QUESTION. APPLY. REFLECT.

Turn a lesson into something useful.

A sample parent-led sequence—not an automated project feature or a measured learning result.

  1. 01

    Choose the idea

    After a story or history reading, ask what makes a source trustworthy. Set a goal: explain a conclusion using evidence.

    YOU SET THE PURPOSE
  2. 02

    Work with Bede

    Use the selected text for guided questions and narration. Listen for what the child can explain in their own words.

    GUIDED PRACTICE
  3. 03

    Try a new context

    Bring two age-appropriate accounts of another event. Ask the learner to compare the evidence and write or tell you which claim is better supported.

    YOU GUIDE THE APPLICATION
  4. 04

    Return to the question

    Ask what would change the conclusion. Revisit the reasoning later with another example—not just the same answer to remember.

ONE HOUSEHOLD. DIFFERENT STAGES.

Meet the learner where they are.

Suggested priorities to adapt with your child—not a fixed curriculum or a promise that every learner progresses at the same rate.

K–2 / FOUNDATIONS

Read, tell, and notice.

Reading foundations, oral narration, handwriting, and number sense. Stay close, read together, and help the child connect words and ideas to familiar experiences.

3–5 / CONNECTIONS

Explain it in your words.

Reading comprehension, spelling and grammar, paragraph writing, and mathematical explanations. Use a written plan, a paper recipe, or a nature journal to apply an idea.

6–8 / REASONING

Give reasons. Consider more.

Analytical reading, structured composition, logic, and mathematical reasoning. Compare interpretations, identify assumptions, and revise a conclusion when the evidence changes.

Homeschool family FAQs

Is there a classical digital program for my eight-year-old in Texas?

Bede is a parent-led classical learning platform for K–8, including reading, writing, and mathematics for an eight-year-old. Texas families can request onboarding around their child's ability, books, and goals. Families arrange school enrollment or homeschooling separately. Review Texas homeschool guidance or request onboarding.

What does a household membership include?

Bede Tutor, Locuto messaging, and the Family Portal, with parent oversight for up to six children. Request current pricing and onboarding details.

Which devices and connection do we need?

Household sessions connect to your home-network host. Away-from-home use needs separately configured, approved access. Voice and handwriting support vary by device and browser.

Are faith subjects required?

No. Scripture & Bible Study and Saints & Catechism are separate, optional subjects; choose either, both, or neither. Saints & Catechism is Catholic. Parents and faith leaders guide spiritual formation; Bede is not a spiritual advisor. Faith and character are never scored.

Can a co-op use a household membership?

Household seats cover one family, not unrelated families or a school. Co-ops and educators arrange a separate offer.

FROM THE FOUNDER

“I named Bede after the Venerable Bede, a Benedictine monk who chronicled the History of the early institutions that formed the Church we know today. The persona also honors a monk who taught me at Portsmouth Abbey, weaving Christian Doctrine, Humanities, and History together in his courses. He was a patient mentor and counselor who taught in the style of Socrates, and he shaped how I teach today.”
Kristian Gonzalez, Founder
