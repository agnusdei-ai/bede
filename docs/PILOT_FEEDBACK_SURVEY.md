# Bede Pilot Feedback Survey

**Audience:** Invited classroom educators and homeschooling parents or guardians.
**When:** After initial setup and after the first supervised instructional session.
**Time:** About 5 minutes.

Thank you for trying Bede. Your feedback helps us improve the setup experience, classical instruction, and teacher- or parent-guided learning. This is a pilot, not a claim of proven learning outcomes.

Do not enter a learner's name, birth date, diagnosis, grades, passwords, or identifying details. Use an approved, nonidentifying session reference if support needs to locate a problem. Follow your school's or family's approved data-sharing process.

## About your session

1. Which setting did you use? **Classroom / Homeschool / Other approved pilot setting**
2. Which role best describes you? **Teacher / Homeschool parent or guardian / Pilot operator**
3. What did you try? **Setup / Learner enrollment / Lesson / Review of learning evidence / Follow-up instruction / Recovery or restart** (select all that apply)

## Setup and access

4. How easy was it to install Bede and reach your workspace? **1 Very difficult – 5 Very easy / Not attempted**
5. Were enrollment, permissions, and next steps clear? **Yes / Partly / No / Not attempted**
6. Did anything prevent you from continuing? **No / Yes**. If yes, describe the step without including private information.

## Instructional experience

7. How well did the lesson fit your intended classical or Charlotte Mason-inspired objective? **1 Not at all – 5 Very well / Not observed**
8. Were the questions, explanations, and pacing appropriate for the learner's current demonstrated understanding? **1 Not at all – 5 Very well / Not observed**
9. Could you understand the evidence behind Bede's suggested next step? **Yes / Partly / No / No suggestion shown**
10. Could you review, change, or decline the suggested follow-up before it affected instruction? **Yes / Partly / No / Not attempted**
11. Did the session help you identify a skill to reinforce or revisit? **Yes / Partly / No / Too early to tell**
12. Compared with your usual preparation and follow-up, how much educator or parent time did Bede save or add? **Saved more than 15 minutes / Saved 5–15 minutes / About the same / Added 5–15 minutes / Added more than 15 minutes / Cannot estimate**

## Trust, accessibility, and improvement

13. Did you encounter inaccurate content, unclear sources, inaccessible controls, unexpected behavior, or an authorization concern? **No / Yes**. If yes, describe the issue without learner-identifying details.
14. How confident were you that you remained in control of instructional decisions? **1 Not confident – 5 Very confident / Not observed**
15. What is the single most important improvement before your next session? **Short response**
16. Would you use Bede again during this supervised pilot? **Yes / Maybe / No**. Why? **Optional short response**

## Optional follow-up

If you want help, contact the pilot coordinator through the approved support channel. Do not put contact information in free-text survey answers.

---

## Operator scoring and release interpretation

- Report classroom and homeschool responses separately; do not compare individual learners or infer a diagnosis or fixed learning style.
- Report response counts, distributions, setup completion, blocked journeys, observed teacher/parent control, and reported time effects.
- Flag any privacy, access-control, or consequential instructional-control concern for immediate engineering review; do not treat survey averages as a substitute for G1/G2/G3 security evidence.
- Pilot survey responses are **in-pilot evidence**, not a prerequisite for Pilot Entry GO.
- Keep real learner records out of this survey. Store and retain responses only under the pilot's approved privacy and retention policy.
