AI Safety, Teacher Control & Access
Model governance and classroom use limitations for Bede

DRAFT - INTERNAL VENDOR REVIEW; NOT EXECUTED




Product role
Bede is an AI-assisted Socratic learning companion intended to work under educator instruction. It asks age-
appropriate questions, supports lessons selected by educators, and presents academic work for human review. It is
not a teacher of record, assessment publisher, school administrator, counselor, or autonomous decision-maker.

Safeguards to validate before any learner-facing release
   Model outputs cannot modify identities, grades, approved curriculum or teacher decisions without a separate
    authorized action.
   Constrain allowed tools, documents and prompts; handle prompt injection and unsafe/off-topic requests with a
    supervised response and escalation route.
   Refuse or abstain when required evidence or provenance is missing; identify drafts as drafts.
   Log human approval/rejection in a versioned, tamper-evident record; test retries, stale decisions and rollback.
   Check educator, campus and learner grants on each protected request and during streaming; revoked users lose
    access.
   No cross-student memory, hidden data reuse, model training on school records or external provider fallback
    unless explicitly authorized.

Claims that must not be made
   Bede does not automatically improve literacy, DIBELS scores or teacher productivity; pilot outcomes must be
    measured, including failures.
   The interface does not make Bede a replacement for teachers or a licensed clinical/psychological assessor.
   AI model policies do not replace the required server permissions, school policies, family rights and technical
    testing.
   A successful unit test or merged PR is not evidence of real-world school deployment readiness.
