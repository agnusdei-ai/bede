BEDE CLASSICAL LEARNING OPERATING SYSTEM

Valor Education · Executive decision brief · Illustrative model

More time for teaching.
Clearer instructional decisions.

Bede unifies the teacher's assessment evidence, Data Day review, educational diagnostics, instructional planning, and documented follow-up. Guided test preparation and original practice materials are part of the broader classical learning vision—not a promise of learner-facing delivery in this pilot.

Assessment accelerator

Capture authorized scores, preserve benchmark and progress purpose, and prepare cited evidence without copying protected test content.

Teacher education support

Present explainable recommendations and a teacher approve/edit/reject workflow; preserve observations and audit history.

Test prep & practice

Plan standards-aligned, original practice and diagnostic follow-up. No official DIBELS passages, forms, score tables, or simulated official tests; learner practice delivery is separately qualified.
