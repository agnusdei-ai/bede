## Access versus remediation

- TTS, enlarged text, tactile diagrams, scribing, dictation, extra time and supported planning can provide access. Independently test comprehension with the agreed support still present; do not count TTS comprehension as decoding improvement.
- Targeted remediation teaches a missing skill through explicit instruction, practice and reassessment. A fraction bridge changes pace and replaces planned work; a reading intervention is not simply shorter homework. Refer needs beyond the service's competence without promising premium specialist entitlement.
- Record separately: comprehension/reasoning, decoding, transcription, executive-function support and generated assistance. Preserve equivalent constructs and permit oral/typed demonstrations where handwriting is not the target. Avoid penalizing assistive technology as “not independent.”
- Actual screen-reader order, mathematical speech, Latin macrons, accessible diagrams, lawful audio, keyboard operation and device usability remain unverified. The history PDF's previously reported certificate issue requires a normal secure access check, not instructions to disable security.

## Workload and credit arithmetic

Five 5h courses + three 2.5h strands = **32.5h/week**; ×36 = **1,170h**; ÷180 = **6.5 planning credits**. Five-day mean = 6.5 engaged hours/day before meals/breaks/travel. Studio is one of the three half-credit strands, not a sixth full credit. Reading, correction, assessment and projects must fit inside these blocks, not create hidden homework. Semester/annual credit policy remains local.

Biology's 1.5h weekly studio totals **54h**. Eighteen designated 1.5h slots total **27h**; the other 27h are related studio. Designated slots themselves include models/data tasks. Neither 54h nor 27h is a claim of bench-experiment hours. Log actual modality and supervision.

The packet shifts substantial work to adults: direct instruction across five subjects, seminar, feedback, practical supervision and troubleshooting. Reusable materials may reduce preparation, but no measured net reduction exists. Family/co-op/school schedules must identify who covers each simultaneous responsibility and substitute when absent.

## Workflow modes, not price tiers

| Proposed mode | Packet / adult role | Handoff and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Ready-to-teach | Reviewed version, objectives/prerequisites, lawful selections, scripts/models, practice/keys, checks, safety/access variants, time/equipment | Adult sees exceptions, makes assessment decisions; not demonstrated as a complete Bede service |
| Co-planning | Same approved spine, bounded pace/context choices with change log | Changes to source, objective, assessment construct or safety invalidate affected review; no unreviewed regeneration |
| Targeted-support | Baseline barrier, focused instruction, access supports, monitored response | Coach/parent receives goal, evidence, action, time, next review and continue/modify/refer/fade criteria |

Concise weekly evidence/exception handoff: packet/version; planned versus actual time; learner demonstration and support conditions; misconception or access barrier; safety/rights exceptions; corrective action/owner; next independent/delayed check. Avoid transcript dumps or unnecessary child information.

Coach handoff additionally records scope/qualification, direct time **and preparation/coordination time**, goal baseline, intervention dosage, progress evidence, retention/transfer and fade/referral criteria. Coaching availability is not emergency cover, clinical care, guaranteed subject expertise or unlimited preparation.

## Full cost and net adult burden

Cash total = Bede service + optional coaching + books/licenses + printing/consumables + allocated equipment + connectivity/device support + travel/access costs + taxes. Keep one-time and recurring costs distinct, allocation denominator real, optional expenses separate and unquoted values **unknown**, not zero.

Adult hours = onboarding + selection/preparation + direct instruction/seminar + lab/studio supervision + marking/review + corrections + technical troubleshooting + coach coordination + access adaptation + recordkeeping. Report initial and recurring hours separately for parents, educators and coaches; capture concurrent care and work shifted between them. Opportunity-cost valuation is optional and must state its rate.

Net burden change compares all those hours against a defined alternative with comparable curriculum and coaching. Do not count reduced lesson drafting while ignoring correction or increased parent teaching. Record abandoned tasks, support failures and learner fatigue, not just successful platform sessions.
