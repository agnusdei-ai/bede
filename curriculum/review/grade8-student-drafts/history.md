### Explicit Teaching

Suggested time: 12 minutes.

Around1450, many societies maintained cities, states and exchange networks; no single place was the centre of all world history. An atlas can locate the Mediterranean, Sahara, Indian Ocean and East Asia, but a modern political map does not show their1450 boundaries.
