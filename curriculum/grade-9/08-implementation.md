## What different adults provide

### Family

Lead adult coordinates schedule, screens resources, leads reading/seminar and low-risk observation, checks work and maintains a portfolio. Budget preparation/feedback and direct instruction honestly using document01. Arrange specialist help when the adult cannot validate algebra, Latin, biology or writing. Do not treat a downloaded answer key as qualified instruction.

### Co-op

Share two meeting days, instructor costs, microscopes/prepared slides and accessible equipment. Name the teaching lead, lab supervisor and substitute; agree on attendance, emergency response, evaluation, record custody and what happens when a specialist is absent. Home work remains part of the same32.5-hour total.

### Charter or other school

Academic lead verifies standards coverage, prerequisites, required credits/seat time or competency evidence, adopted resources and nondevotional suitability. Special-education/accessibility staff verify supports and actual digital materials. IT/privacy leads review data flow, tools, age/consent, device access, network/retention and vendor agreements. Procurement approves contracting/payment/insurance requirements under the operator's policies.

This curriculum **does not change** the current teacher-only, synthetic-only Bede school-pilot scope. Use documents as adult review materials unless a separately qualified deployment and signed scope permit more. No real student upload, automatic grading or transcript workflow is authorized by this package.

## Inclusion and support choices

Choose a modest core shelf and shared instruments before extensions. Print reading/annotations to reduce device time; use computers in focused scheduled studio turns. Offer dictated planning or speech-to-text, accessible graphs, adapted movement and partner measurements where appropriate. Preserve independent reasoning; record when the observed skill differs from the original task. Obtain professional support rather than promising this package resolves all access barriers.
