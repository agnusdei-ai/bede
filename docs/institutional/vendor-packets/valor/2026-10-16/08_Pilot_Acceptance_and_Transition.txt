Entry criteria - synthetic stage
   Signed or expressly approved evaluation scope naming roles, allowed synthetic data, use locations, endpoints,
    permitted model/provider and start/stop authority.
   Sandbox environment contains no real education records, staff secrets, student accounts or production
    credentials.
   Technical operator can stop access and recover synthetic environments; known gaps disclosed to School.
   Named School instructional/IT/security contacts; approved test plan, dates and communication methods.

Criteria before a real-student pilot
   FERPA/COPPA/Texas privacy analysis and executable DPA/contract with named subprocessors, parental-consent
    plan and data deletion terms.
   G1 tenant isolation and G2 teacher authorization pass on an exact release build with campus/learner negative
    cases; no 71 ABSENT in in-scope mandatory cells.
   WebAuthn enrollment, administrative recovery, staff offboarding, session and stream revocation validated on
    real compatible devices.
   G3 installation, encrypted backup, destructive restore, update/rollback and measured throughput pass within
    approved hardware limits.
   School-approved content/curriculum, accessibility evidence and incident response contacts are recorded.
   Separate dated engineering evidence acceptance and School real-data GO with maximum cohort, capacity,
    monitoring and stop triggers.

Stop, incident and recovery triggers
Suspend participation immediately upon cross-campus disclosure, revoked-account access, failed fail-closed response,
unexpected provider egress, unauthorized real data ingestion or uncontained security incident. School and Vendor
agree the notification/escalation window, responsible owner and preservation of evidence. Resumption requires
documented remediation, a new tested build when applicable, and explicit joint approval.

Exit and handoff checklist
   Disable test identities, invitations, sessions, streams, model access and tokens.
   Export agreed School-provided artifacts (none containing student records in the synthetic stage); verify database,
    logs and backups against the retention plan.
   Return equipment and revoke VPN/domain/API permissions where applicable.
   Issue outcome memo including what worked, negative results, unresolved safety gaps, measured cost/capacity
    and recommendation.
   If proceeding, negotiate a separate signed paid agreement and DPA; never convert the free synthetic stage
    automatically.
