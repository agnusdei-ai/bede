## Week 1: Attention, lines and starting routines

Lesson: `g8-year-wider_learning-w01` · Draft version 1

### Learning focus

Apply and explain attention, lines and starting routines using the specified independent and transfer tasks.

### Before starting

Describe a simple feature and follow a short paper plan; provide access supports.

### Explicit Teaching

Suggested time: 12 minutes.

Art: draw five different lines and describe how direction changes emphasis. Nature: separate observation from inference in an invented leaf sketch. Practical: list materials before starting a paper task. Wellbeing: plan breaks and an accessible daily movement routine with an adult. Continue four short weekly strand blocks rather than treating this focus as the only arts, nature, practical or movement time in the week.
Entry check: ask the learner to demonstrate the stated prerequisite with a fresh example. If missing, teach that skill first; do not treat age or an earlier week number as evidence of readiness.
