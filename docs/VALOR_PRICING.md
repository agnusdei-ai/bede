## Commercial offers

| Offer | Price | Eligibility / term | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Controlled pilot | **$950 total** | Proposed 6–8 weeks, approximately 15–25 learners | Fixed pilot fee; pilot size and duration to be finalized with campus |
| Per-student campus | **$25 per learner per month** | Approximately 25–100 learners, 25-seat minimum; **12-month commitment**, monthly billing permitted | Recommended for small campuses and controlled expansion |
| Founding campus | **$2,700 per campus per month** | Unlimited learner seats at the licensed campus; **12-month commitment**; promotional enrollment window: **Fall 2026–Spring 2027** | Preferred from ~108 learners onward; committed promotional founding-school rate for eligible memberships in the specified window |

At 108 licensed learners, 108 × $25 = $2,700/month. At or above that point, the unlimited founding-campus offer is economically preferable. The 101–107 learner band can remain on the per-seat plan until the crossover, subject to campus-specific negotiations. The 25-student floor implies a $625/month minimum on the per-seat plan.

The $2,700/month founding-school membership is an approved promotional offer for eligible campus agreements entered during Fall 2026 through Spring 2027. The agreed price applies throughout each signed 12-month subscription term, even when that term extends beyond Spring 2027. Eligibility after the promotional enrollment window and subsequent renewal rates require their own approved offer terms; the founding price is not automatically indefinite.\n\nAll prices are USD; campus-specific taxes, hardware, and separately scoped professional services are excluded unless explicitly agreed. Do not present pilot pricing as a recurring monthly charge. These prices are decisions to preserve for future proposal and contract drafting, not evidence that a campus has accepted them.

## Included product scope

- Student Socratic learning companion with authorized instructional content.
- Teacher AI workspace: lesson support, learner pods, instructional review, and educator-approved interventions.
- Progress reporting: learner evidence, classroom summaries, campus insights, and permitted parent reporting; preserve provenance and human judgment.
- Educator identity/role boundaries, campus-scoped authorization, and privacy controls.
- Limited standard remote onboarding, setup guidance, initial roster configuration, standard reports, and product support. Define support SLAs before contracting.

No separate recurring fee for standard teacher tools, progress reporting, educator access controls, or the limited onboarding above. Custom SIS/LMS connectors, bespoke curriculum work, onsite services, unlimited coaching, and custom infrastructure changes are excluded and require separate scope/approval.

## Appliance / deployment assumptions

Apple Silicon Mac mini hardware is sold separately from software licensing. Proposed baselines: 32 GB unified memory / 1 TB storage for smaller deployments; 64 GB / 4 TB for larger campuses. These configurations are **not** approved concurrency or capacity guarantees. Qualify sustained student/educator concurrency, model throughput, response latency, backup/recovery, update procedure, and security before committing campus capacity.

## Pilot acceptance / contract guardrails

Before broader institutional release, verify teacher assignment-scoped access, student privacy and tenant separation, approved curriculum grounding, teacher override, evidence-backed reporting calculations, local inference/data-flow restrictions, backup/restore, security tests, and representative appliance load testing.

Do not imply official DIBELS 8 measures from Bede conversational data; integrate only authorized, provenance-preserving assessment records where relevant. Comprehension may be assessed through academic evidence; compassion and conscience should not be converted into AI-generated moral/character scores.
