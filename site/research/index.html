THE METHOD BEHIND GUIDED LEARNING

Research & Findings

An adult sets the direction. The learner does the thinking. Technology supports the work.

What the research supports

Parents as learning guides

A 2006 review of 37 studies found generally positive effects when parents tutored their children, mostly in primary-grade reading.

Focused tutoring and thoughtful practice

Research reviews find positive learning effects from structured tutoring. Instructional guidance also supports recalling, explaining, and revisiting ideas over time.

AI beside a human tutor

A 2024 Tutor CoPilot trial, reported in a working paper, found a 4-percentage-point increase in lesson-topic mastery when AI supported human tutors.

AI-assisted learning in practice

Harvard University

A 194-student undergraduate physics study found stronger immediate learning with a research-designed AI tutor than with in-class active learning.

First Avenue School, Newark

The school has documented using Khanmigo alongside educators in grades 5–8.

Independent examples; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Keep the adult in charge

Set clear goals. Prompt thinking before offering answers. Ask the learner to explain, return to the book, and practice independently. Check understanding again later.

Results vary with the learner, setting, and implementation.
