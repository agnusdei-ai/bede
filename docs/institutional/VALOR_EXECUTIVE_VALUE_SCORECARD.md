# Valor executive value case — Bede teacher-only pilot

> **Bede returns time to teaching.** Turn assessment results into cited Data Day evidence, teacher-approved plans, and measurable instructional follow-up—without automatic learner delivery or a new paid AI ingestion dependency.

**Status:** Illustrative economics; not actual Valor costs or validated savings. Synthetic-only pilot. No student-facing tutoring, billing activation, or Pilot GO.

## Outbound-material standard

Use this concise heading and two-sentence explanation in **new** Valor executive briefs, demonstration forms, proposal summaries, and printable handoffs:

**Bede | More time for teaching. Clearer instructional decisions.**

Bede brings assessment evidence, Data Day preparation, instructional recommendations, and teacher-approved planning into one classical educator workspace. Measure administrative time recovered, protect teacher authority, and control ongoing technology expense.

Accompany any financial figure with its period, inputs, evidence status, and **cash savings / avoided future spending / capacity released** classification. Do not label staff-time value as operating-profit improvement unless it actually reduces paid payroll or a budgeted expense.

## The executive KPI scorecard

| KPI | Definition and source | Baseline and measurement | Proposed acceptance target (not a claim) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher prep minutes per completed Data Day case | Timed teacher effort: gather assessment → evidence packet → recommendation review → decision/plan | Pre-pilot manual sample vs Bede sample; equal case mix | At least 30% less median hands-on time |
| Teacher decision fidelity | % of Bede recommendations explicitly approved/edited/rejected, with citation, decision, reason and plan recorded | Audit-backed synthetic pilot; later authorized evaluation | 100% recorded; zero automatic approvals |
| Evidence integrity | % cases with complete instrument, form, purpose, status, grade/window and source provenance | Record validation + teacher review | 100% required fields or explicit not-applicable status |
| Operational corrections | Correction/re-entry events per 100 reviewed cases | Compare existing teacher workflow and pilot; exclude instrument content | Decreasing trend; zero silent composite from missing measures |
| Recovered teaching capacity | Verified time saved × loaded labor rate | Sampled minutes and district-approved rate | Report hours first; monetize as opportunity value |
| Avoidable paid overtime / contractor spend | Approved invoices or payroll reduction directly attributable to workflow | Finance-led baseline vs actual | Report only realized, booked cash impact |
| Cost per completed reviewed case | Total incremental Bede software, hardware amortization, power, IT labor ÷ reviewed cases | Expense ledger + run logs | At or below pre-agreed threshold |
| External LLM ingestion charge | External tokens billed × rate | Vendor/egress logs and invoices | **$0** for locally isolated pilot |
| Campus total cost of ownership | Bede license + hardware depreciation/amortization + power + operations + support + training + integration | Finance ledger / procurement contract | Under quantified cash savings + budgeted capacity value, with categories separately disclosed |
| Teacher satisfaction and clarity | Post-task questionnaire and observed independent task completion | Same teachers complete standard scenarios | ≥80% complete without developer help; no unresolved critical accessibility issue |

**Data collection:** capture 10–20 representative synthetic assessment/teacher cases per participating teacher when feasible. Record elapsed wall-clock and actual hands-on minutes separately, benchmark vs progress monitoring and case complexity, then repeat after familiarization. Do not treat sample estimates as statistically proven impact.

## Example annual campus economics (ILLUSTRATIVE, USD)

Assumptions, *not* Valor actuals: 30 teachers; 36 teaching weeks; 1.5 hours per teacher per week of **verified** administrative time recovered; $42/hour fully loaded labor cost; $2,600/month founding-campus license; $3,000 hardware cash purchase; $600 electricity/year; $2,400 incremental IT support/year. Hardware depreciated over 3 years straight-line for illustrative book expense; actual accounting treatment and useful life require Valor finance approval.

| Item | Formula | Year 1 USD | Classification |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Teacher hours released | 30 × 1.5 × 36 | 1,620 hours | Capacity, not necessarily cash |
| Teacher time opportunity value | 1,620 × $42 | $68,040 | **Capacity-value estimate only** |
| Software subscription | $2,600 × 12 | ($31,200) | Recurring cash OPEX |
| Hardware acquisition | Assumed initial purchase | ($3,000) | Cash / potentially capital expenditure |
| Electricity | Assumed annual consumption | ($600) | Recurring cash OPEX |
| Incremental IT support | Assumed incremental annual cost | ($2,400) | Recurring cash OPEX |
| **Year-one incremental cash outlay** | 31,200 + 3,000 + 600 + 2,400 | **($37,200)** | Cash spending |
| **Illustrative capacity value minus cash outlay** | 68,040 − 37,200 | **$30,840** | Non-GAAP planning comparison, **not profit/cash savings** |
| Annual book expense with three-year hardware depreciation | 31,200 + 1,000 + 600 + 2,400 | ($35,200) | Illustrative P&L expense excluding tax/financing |
| External AI/token ingestion bill | Local inference only, egress blocked | $0 | Vendor inference charges only; excludes electricity, compute/support |

At these assumptions, recovered time needed to offset first-year cash spend on a **capacity-value basis** is $37,200 / ($42 × 30 × 36) = **0.82 hours per teacher per week**. This is **not cash break-even**. Finance should calculate actual savings separately.

### Finance-recognized avoided-cost example

Only if documented: 12 hours/month of previously paid administrative overtime avoided × $35/hour × 12 months = **$5,040 annual cash expenditure avoided**. Evidence: approved payroll rate, historical paid overtime, comparable periods, and attributable scheduling decisions. Do **not** simultaneously monetize those same hours as $42/hour teacher capacity.

### Accounting classification and cost-avoidance ledger

| Economic category | Record and proof | Income statement / cash flow meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher hours reallocated | Time-study and timetable; no actual reduction in paid hours | Capacity / service quality KPI; **not** recognized savings |
| Vacant position or new contractor genuinely not added | Approved staffing plan and canceled/avoided purchase order | Forecast cost avoidance only, not booked historical expense reduction |
| Paid overtime/contractor invoices actually reduced | Payroll, AP records, comparable workload and causal explanation | Realized OPEX saving relative to validated baseline |
| Software licensing | Signed subscription and AP ledger | OPEX over service period |
| Hardware | Purchase invoice and capitalization policy | Capex/cash outflow if capitalized; subsequent depreciation expense |
| Local compute and electricity | Meter/host use and utility rate | Operating expense, including local inference |
| Support and training | IT time sheets and supplier invoices | OPEX or allocated internal effort; disclose assumptions |
| External model tokens/ingestion | Usage/export/egress records and invoices | $0 external API bill **only when actually isolated and verified** |
| Data migration or integration | Implementation effort and professional services | One-time cost; include in total-cost model when incurred |

**Avoid double counting.** Never sum recovered-capacity valuation with overtime avoided for the same labor hours. Reconcile all actual expense claims with Valor Finance/Controller.

## Report format for executives

**Baseline → post-use → delta → attribution → finance classification → evidence link.**

Example: median Data Day preparation **60 → 36 minutes**, a **24-minute / 40%** reduction (hypothetical only). Report teacher time released in hours per reporting period. Separately state realized reductions in paid overtime, external vendors, administrative headcount growth avoided (forecast only), incremental software and equipment, and power/support.

### Pilot decision gates
1. Complete teacher-only assessment/Data Day/approve-edit-reject/plan/observation workflow, delivery state `NOT_REQUESTED`, and persistence/revocation evidence.
2. Physical Mac G3 install, restart, passkey and recovery with synthetic data.
3. Demonstrate time study using comparable tasks and show effective local AI/egress isolation.
4. Finance approves budget inputs and savings classifications.
5. Owner issues separate Pilot GO. Commercial ROI figures remain illustrative until measured.
