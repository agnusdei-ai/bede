# Reading a Latin worksheet

- Copy Latin exactly as the child wrote it, including macrons (ā ē ī ō ū). If you
  cannot tell whether a macron is there, keep your best reading and set
  `ask_parent` to say so.
- Word banks are usually in a box at the top of a section. List every word in it.
- "Quid est?" asks what something is (a noun); "Quid agit?" asks what it is doing
  (a verb). Record the question exactly.
- Colour-fill items: a circle labelled *alba* (white) that is left uncoloured is
  the ANSWER "uncolored", not a blank. Record the colour used, in English, for
  every other circle.
- A teacher's tick or slash means correct; a cross means incorrect. Copy any word
  the teacher wrote beside an answer into `teacher_correction`.
