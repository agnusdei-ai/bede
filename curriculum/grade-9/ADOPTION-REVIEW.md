## Commercial-use decision

The [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) permits noncommercial sharing/adaptation with attribution and ShareAlike; it does **not** by itself clear a paid proprietary adaptation. Statutory exceptions and any separately acquired rights require use-specific assessment. Do not infer that all fee-based teaching is prohibited, or that merely linking makes every commercial use permissible.

All three current OpenStax prefaces explicitly direct commercial users to the [content reuse request form](https://openstax.org/r/content-reuse-request). Their reuse notices also require prior written permission for ingestion into LLMs or generative-AI offerings, not just model training. **Do not ingest these resources into Bede retrieval, embeddings, prompts or a curriculum-generation corpus on the basis of free web access.** This records publisher notice, not a legal determination of its reach. No form has been submitted.

**Owner decision: replace, not request permission.** OpenStax dependencies have been removed from active readings and remapped to independently licensed sources and original biology notes. No publisher outreach is authorized or needed for the excluded material. The earlier permission-inquiry proposal is superseded. Third-party licenses still apply to the replacement resources; linking does not turn them into Bede-owned content.

If permission is declined, too costly, or incompatible with proprietary delivery, select an independently rights-cleared alternative **before** adoption. A purchased book grants lawful possession/access, not automatic copying, adaptation, redistribution or AI rights. Older CC BY copies may have independently valid grants, but none has been authenticated here; do not relabel a current edition from an old license claim.

Replacement requires a crosswalk: old week/objective/section → new edition/section → reading load → exercises and worked keys → assessment alignment → accessible format → rights evidence → specialist approval. Update documents 02–05 and relevant biology references together; rerun the package validator. No replacement or remapping is claimed complete.

The [Project Gutenberg license](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html) distinguishes public-domain text from its trademark/license wrapper, warns that catalogs can be wrong, and requires checking the ebook's own notice. Commercial distribution retaining its branding carries additional conditions. Choose and document the lawful distribution route and territory; do not blanket-label all downloaded files or modern additions unrestricted.
