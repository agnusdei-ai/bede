## Instructional commitments to evaluate, not slogans

Substantial texts; narration, discussion, composition and revision; grammar and argument; sourcing/context/corroboration/interpretation; chronology/geography; mathematics and scientific inquiry; nature, arts, practical work and movement. Use manageable lessons and explicit modeling/practice when needed, with progressively less unnecessary prompting.

Charlotte Mason's historical practices are primary-source history, not proof that all modern adaptations or software are effective. Modern explicit instruction, accessible media, coding and measured support-fading must be described as adaptations. Do not claim classical superiority.

Assess comprehension separately from decoding; reasoning separately from expression/transcription/spelling; knowledge separately from executive-function performance. Accommodations provide access; remediation explicitly teaches an identified skill. Neither diagnoses nor cures ADHD/dyslexia, and no fixed learning-style labels are adopted.
