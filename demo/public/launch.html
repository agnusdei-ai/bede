Got Questions? Got Bede? 😎

A few honest answers for thoughtful homeschool parents.

Does Bede replace the parent?

No, and it is built so that it cannot. You choose the subjects, set the pace, and decide what your children study. Bede tutors within the plan you set and reports back to you. The parent is the teacher; Bede is the assistant who sits with one child while you work with another.

Is this just more screen time?

Sessions are short by design, with breaks built in and a daily limit you control. Much of the work happens off the screen entirely — reading real books, narrating aloud, writing and drawing by hand, and going outside to observe.

The summer program is deliberately the least screen-based part of the year: children go out, look closely, and come back to tell Bede what they saw.

Is this an AI school?

No. There is no enrollment, no accreditation, no classroom, and no teacher of record. You are homeschooling. Bede is a tool you bring to the work you are already doing, the way you would bring a curriculum or a tutor.

What is included?

Every membership includes all three: Bede, the Socratic tutor; Locuto, secure messaging for your family and co-op; and the Family Portal, where you plan the year and see how it is going. Parent tools, oversight, and verified access come with all of it. One membership covers up to six children in your household, at one price.

Is curriculum included?

Bede Classical Core is included — a full classical scope across the subjects Bede teaches. If your family already uses a particular publisher, Bede works alongside it, and publisher curriculum editions are available separately.

What about sports and extracurriculars?

These are not part of the core program and we do not run them. The Co-op Portal is where trusted families coordinate local leagues, clubs, lessons, and other opportunities among themselves.

How does verified access work?

Locuto and the Co-op Portal are closed communities, not public feeds. Access is granted by the co-op or family that invited you, and can be withdrawn by them. There is no open directory, no discovery, and no way for a stranger to reach your children.
