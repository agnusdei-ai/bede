THE BEDE PROMISE, IN PRACTICE

Learn deeply. Use what you learn.

Across the K–8 years, learning takes shape through close attention, clear expression, thoughtful judgment, and useful work.

  • 01Read closely. Notice meaning, sequence, and evidence in a text.
  • 02Write clearly. Put an observation, explanation, or argument into the learner’s own words.
  • 03Think through new scenarios. Bring ideas together, ask what follows, and explain a choice.
  • 04Apply knowledge in everyday work. Carry a lesson into a practical task, conversation, or decision.

GUIDED BY AN ADULT

Parents and educators choose the reading and practice, review the learner’s practical work, and discuss what it shows.

FROM THE LESSON TO LIFE

“Where could I use this?”

Illustrative, adult-led activities—not automated Bede features or measured results. Adjust the task to the learner's readiness.

K–2   /   NOTICE & EXPLAIN

A story becomes a choice.

After a read-aloud, retell a character's decision. Draw or write what happened. Discuss a different choice in a familiar situation.

Ask: “What in the story makes you think that?”

3–5   /   CONNECT & APPLY

Fractions become a plan.

Use a recipe on paper to work out half or twice the quantities. Explain the calculations in a short paragraph, then check them with an adult.

Ask: “How could you check your answer another way?”

6–8   /   REASON & REVISE

An observation tests a claim.

Compare a claim about plant growth with observations in a nature journal. Write an explanation, consider another cause, and revise the conclusion if needed.

Ask: “What evidence would change your mind?”

Look for application, not just completion.

Can the learner explain without copying, choose a useful approach, support a conclusion, and use the idea in an unfamiliar example? Review an initial attempt, a later attempt, and a delayed check. These are suggested adult review questions, not a validated scoring system.

FORMATION, THROUGH THE WORK OF LEARNING

Timeless virtues.
Put into practice.

A learner grows through what they read, notice, question, and do—with a thoughtful adult beside them. Bede supports the conversation; the family or educator directs it.

01ComprehensionExplain what the text says.

02CompassionRespond with patience and care.

03ConscienceBring learning into life.

A human practice, not a score

With a real book open and an adult guiding the exchange, virtue becomes visible in the habits of learning.

  1. 01

    Courage

    Invite the learner to give a reason, even when they are unsure.

  2. 02

    Humility

    Make room to revise a first thought when the evidence changes.

  3. 03

    Wonder

    Ask what a story helps us notice about the world and one another.

  4. 04

    Attentiveness

    Read closely together before offering a response.

  5. 05

    Honesty

    Distinguish what the text supports from what we are guessing.

  6. 06

    Gratitude

    Name who and what helped us learn along the way.

  7. 07

    Perseverance

    Return to a difficult passage or make another thoughtful draft.

  8. 08

    Kindness

    Practice disagreeing respectfully—and listening to the answer.
