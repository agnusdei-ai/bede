# Grades 9–12 beta target

The owner confirmed **November 20, 2026** as the target date and explicitly
selected **complete four full-year curricula**, not a starter release.
Grades 9–12 take priority over unfinished Grade 8. Both homeschool/co-op and
charter-school tutoring must be supported.

This records the requested target, not a promise that the outstanding work
can pass review overnight. Do not silently narrow delivery to starter units,
call source acquisition course completion, or activate unverified lessons.

## Completion evidence required per declared course

- Full-year sequence with actual teaching, bounded readings and prerequisites.
- Student instructions, explicit models, guided practice and independent tasks.
- Bede scaffolding that guides active tasks without disclosing assessment keys.
- Separate educator keys, rubrics, misconceptions and instructional responses.
- Immediate, delayed and unfamiliar-application checks.
- Source-specific permissions, attribution, retained license obligations.
- Accessible alternatives, safe practical routes and adult delivery guidance.
- Exact-version review and verification evidence, plus honest unresolved gates.

Use existing grade completion tasks and the existing factory rather than
creating another curriculum engine. The older Grade 8-first dependency is
superseded for content authoring; Grade 8 is not thereby complete.

## Current release decision

**Not ready for a complete Grades 9–12 curriculum release.**
Grade 9 retains nine authored teaching weeks and Week 10 academic extensions
in all six subjects. Other later courses remain unfinished; the downloaded resources
are instructional inputs, not complete Bede teaching packages.

The owner subsequently authorized ordered PR publication and requires
completed content live in both demo and enrolled Bede. Source-only PRs do not
meet that objective. Verified integration and release remain necessary;
real learner trials and unsupported availability claims are not authorized.
