A COMPANION, NOT ANOTHER CURRICULUM TO SERVE

Keep the relationship.
Get support for the work.

Bede brings lesson planning, an AI-assisted Socratic companion, learning records, and progress together. You choose the books, goals, subjects, and what happens next.

Prepare a purposeful day

Choose each child's subjects, grade, pacing, and texts. Use the plan to support your family's day rather than moving every part of it onto a screen.

Hear the learner think

Bede asks questions instead of simply supplying answers. Oral narration and written work give you something meaningful to discuss together.

Decide with context

Review the session summary and academic records. You decide whether an idea needs another explanation, more practice, or a new use beyond the lesson.

READ. QUESTION. APPLY. REFLECT.

Turn a lesson into something useful.

A sample parent-led sequence—not an automated project feature or a measured learning result.

  1. 01

    Choose the idea

    After a story or history reading, ask what makes a source trustworthy. Set a goal: explain a conclusion using evidence.

    YOU SET THE PURPOSE
  2. 02

    Work with Bede

    Use the selected text for guided questions and narration. Listen for what the child can explain in their own words.

    GUIDED PRACTICE
  3. 03

    Try a new context

    Bring two age-appropriate accounts of another event. Ask the learner to compare the evidence and write or tell you which claim is better supported.

    YOU GUIDE THE APPLICATION
  4. 04

    Return to the question

    Ask what would change the conclusion. Revisit the reasoning later with another example—not just the same answer to remember.

    REFLECTION & RETENTION

The aim is focused practice, thoughtful feedback, and knowledge the child can use. Bede has not yet demonstrated faster learning or improved transfer in an independent outcome study. See the human-led learning approach.

ONE HOUSEHOLD. DIFFERENT STAGES.

Meet the learner where they are.

Suggested priorities to adapt with your child—not a fixed curriculum or a promise that every learner progresses at the same rate.

K–2 / FOUNDATIONS

Read, tell, and notice.

Reading foundations, oral narration, handwriting, and number sense. Stay close, read together, and help the child connect words and ideas to familiar experiences.

3–5 / CONNECTIONS

Explain it in your words.

Reading comprehension, spelling and grammar, paragraph writing, and mathematical explanations. Use a written plan, a paper recipe, or a nature journal to apply an idea.

6–8 / REASONING

Give reasons. Consider more.

Analytical reading, structured composition, logic, and mathematical reasoning. Compare interpretations, identify assumptions, and revise a conclusion when the evidence changes.

ONE TABLE, MANY TRADITIONS

Your family's beliefs stay yours.

Our roots are Catholic; Christian families of every tradition are welcome. Scripture & Bible Study and Saints & Catechism are separate, optional subjects. Enable either, both, or neither.

Saints & Catechism is explicitly Catholic. Bede is not a spiritual advisor, and a child's faith or character is never scored. Parents and their own pastor, priest, or minister lead spiritual formation.

MOBILE, TABLET & DESKTOP

Fit the device to the moment.

Use a supported mobile browser for a short conversation, a compatible tablet for voice or handwritten work, and a desktop for planning and review. Keep the real book, paper, and parent within the learning experience.

On-the-go sessions require access to your approved installation. Standard household use connects to your home-network host; access from elsewhere needs separate, approved configuration. This is not an offline tutoring or automatic cross-device session-handoff promise. Voice, microphone permissions, and handwriting support vary by device and browser.
