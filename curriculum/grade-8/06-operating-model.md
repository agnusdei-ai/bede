# Seminar/studio proposal and bounded evaluation

## Proposed lesson architecture — not a current product claim

Use a common reviewed academic spine with explicit explanation/modeling, human-led seminar and practical studio, supported practice, independent demonstrations and delayed retrieval/transfer. Bede should reduce repetitive preparation and coordination **without replacing it with continual prompt-writing, draft repair and approval work**.

One common lesson and approved adaptation rules should be easier to verify than unrelated per-learner generated curricula. Adaptations may change presentation, pacing, access and support while preserving the assessed construct. Substantive content, evidence, safety or assessment changes require new version-bound human approval; trivial formatting changes need not trigger needless academic reapproval. The approved factory specification must define that boundary.

### Weekly reviewed-packet acceptance checklist

Objectives/IDs and prerequisites; exact readings/editions/locators/rights; prior retrieval; vocabulary/background; misconceptions and explicit worked examples; materials/safety; narration/discussion/seminar prompts; supported practice and independent tasks; rubric/exemplars/answer guidance; bounded accessible variants; delayed retention/transfer checks; prioritized exception summary; goal-based coaching handoff; version/reviewer provenance.

Source models carry some IDs/status/review/variant foundations, but **a complete schema-to-delivery workflow for this packet was not verified**. Do not build a second factory here. Hand this acceptance list to the queued production specification/implementation/slice work.

## Workflow modes — not pricing tiers

- **Ready-to-teach:** deliver a reviewed packet with accurate materials and minimal setup. Adult can inspect substantive assumptions and see prioritized exceptions.
- **Co-planning:** preserve the educator's approved sequence/books; Bede assists bounded preparation and logistics for human selection/review.
- **Targeted support:** identify a specific observed barrier, teach/support a routine or skill and check progress; refer to qualified help when indicated.

Parent-directed service does not mean Bede supplies a school staff. Coaching availability, qualifications, capacity, prices and entitlement allocations remain unknown as recorded in document01. Essential safety/access supports are not premium-only.

## Coaching handoff record — proposed fields

Goal/objective; baseline and independent evidence; barrier stated without diagnosis; accommodation versus remediation; proposed action and required staff qualification; direct contact minutes; preparation/coordination minutes; learner/family action; evidence source and assessment construct; success criteria; retention/transfer check; next review; human decision to **continue/modify/refer/fade**; owner/date/version; minimum retention/privacy classification.

Preserve learner agency: independence means competent use of appropriate supports, not denying a screen reader, scribe or needed intervention. Fade redundant prompts only when performance evidence supports it.

## Net educator-relief measurement

Report tasks removed **minus** work added, plus who receives displaced labor:

```
net adult time relief =
  comparable preparation/coordination time removed
  − onboarding/training
  − source checking and rights review
  − verification/correction and draft repair
  − accessibility adaptation
  − data entry/reconciliation
  − troubleshooting
  − coach coordination
  − ongoing maintenance
```

Use repeated time logs/task sampling, not favorable anecdotes. Separate one-time setup from steady-state recurring work. Report teacher, parent, coach, academic/rights reviewer and operations time independently, then total them; moving work to parents is not a system saving. Include family rescue time, out-of-session help and replacement specialist costs.

Measure exhaustion/burnout separately from minutes. Select a rights-cleared appropriate instrument with qualified review, voluntary confidential participation and restricted reporting; do not diagnose or combine exhaustion scores with child grades. No instrument or license selected in this baseline.

## Future affordability model

Include texts/licensing, printing, labs/supervision, devices/repair/connectivity, subscriptions, AI/model usage, adult preparation/contact/review, coaching, accessibility, remediation/intervention, onboarding, coordination and maintenance. Distinguish cash cost from unpaid labor/opportunity cost and required from optional items. Model realistic low/base/high cases and shared-cost allocation using confirmed cohort assumptions. No new price, tier, savings claim or paid purchase is authorized.

## Future evaluation design — proposal only

Proposed conditions:

1. Existing practice.
2. Reviewed reusable materials plus comparable human support.
3. **The same reviewed materials/support** plus bounded Bede orchestration.

If sample is limited, prioritize2vs3 to estimate orchestration's incremental contribution. Keep coaching, essential access supports and indicated interventions comparable; if coaching differs, label the result a combined-package effect. Never remove accommodations to create a clean control.

A roughly eight-week stage may explore feasibility and workflow errors; duration alone cannot establish learning preservation or efficacy. Before participants: independent statistical/design review, legitimate consent/authorization, data minimization/retention plan, legal/access/safety review, independent scoring and predetermined stop rules. No sample recruited or pilot launched here.

### Outcomes and guardrails to specify before launch

- Independent **new** writing and math tasks with human/blinded scoring; do not reuse practiced prompts as transfer evidence.
- Historical inquiry using a new source: context, sourcing, corroboration, interpretation.
- Delayed retention/transfer at prespecified times;7–14/28days are proposals, not validated thresholds.
- Prompting/support required, appropriate access-tool use, completion quality and rescue time.
- Access barriers, unsafe/inaccurate outputs, citation errors, review escapes, correction/repair effort.
- Net workload/cost across all adults; exhaustion measured separately.
- Predefined success/failure/stop thresholds and missing-data handling.

Statistical nonsignificance of a decline in a small sample **does not prove preserved learning**. A noninferiority claim needs a justified margin, adequate design/power and analysis; causal claims need appropriate assignment/comparison and uncertainty. Feasibility findings, academic readiness, pilot-evaluated status and efficacy are separate states.

## Completion versus publication

Fully authored, academically reviewed, pilot-evaluated, publication-approved and live-activated are separate, version-bound states. The owner-approved sequence is end-to-end Grade8, then separately Grade9→10→11→12, not simultaneous generation. This baseline reports missing evidence and hands off to the approved pipeline; it does not advance those states.
