One Table, Many Traditions

Built for Christian families, whatever your church

Agnus Dei's roots are Catholic — the founder's, and the Venerable Bede's. That is a history, not a gate. Bede is built so a Baptist family, a Catholic family, a non-denominational family, and an Anglican family can each sit down to the same tutor and get faith formation that sounds like their own home, not somebody else's. Not a watered-down middle. Your own.

Scripture & Bible Study

Bible narrative, memory verses, and the moral law the text plainly teaches. Deliberately denomination-neutral: Bede will not assert one tradition's doctrine, canon, or ordering as though every Christian holds it.

Saints & Catechism

The Catholic module, and openly so — saints, catechism, and virtue formation drawn from a real published catechism series. Unchanged, undiluted, and entirely optional.

You Choose Which, or Neither

These are two independent subjects, not one relabeled. Enable either, both, or neither per student. Nothing about the rest of the curriculum depends on your answer.

Your Words, Not Ours

Tell Bede your family's church tradition in one short field and it frames Scripture, Saints, and Morning Time to fit — without assuming devotional practice you don't keep. Pick the Bible your family already reads at the kitchen table, and Bede matches its wording when it quotes.

KJV NKJV ESV NIV NASB NLT CSB RSV-CE NABRE NRSV-CE Douay-Rheims

Where Bede stops, on purpose. Bede is not a spiritual advisor and must never become one. Doctrine, moral counsel, and pastoral judgment stay with you and with your own pastor, priest, deacon, or minister. When a child asks something that belongs to them, Bede says so and sends it home.

Bede also never scores, measures, or quantifies your child's faith. There is no engagement metric on prayer, no progress bar on belief, and there never will be — that is written into Bede's constitution as a rule, not left to a setting. A child's spiritual life comes from the child, not from a number.

Unify the Fragments

The Bede Difference

Most digital tools treat learning like a factory line: rigid and fragmented. Bede works differently. Guide your child through the Trivium in one place, with real books in hand and your judgment always in charge.

The Current Chaos

  • Rigid and closed. Mass market apps lock families into one fixed path, leaving little room to guide your own child.
  • App fatigue. Separate tools for reading, narration, and progress tracking force parents to juggle logins instead of teaching.
  • Algorithmic chaos. General chatbots hallucinate. They ignore children's needs and carry no formation of their own.
  • Screens instead of books. The device becomes the curriculum, and the shelf of real books quietly stops getting opened.
  • Your child as the product. Learning data leaves the house, and you never quite learn where it went or who reads it.

The Bede Freedom

  • Your books, your pace. Choose the subjects, texts, and topics each day. Bede's character and safety rules stay fixed. No one changes them quietly, not even us.
  • One guided session. Bede blends Socratic dialogue, narration, and a parent summary into a single sitting, subject by subject.
  • Built for children, on purpose. Bede runs in an environment you host and encrypt yourself. It moderates content in real time, protecting family learning.
  • The book stays central. Tell Bede what you are reading together and it anchors the conversation there — Aesop and Kipling in the early years, Shakespeare, Austen, Dickens, Plutarch, and Chesterton by the end of eighth grade.
  • A record you actually own. Narration scores, mastery, and session summaries live in your own encrypted database, and one button deletes a student's data completely.

Core Capabilities

How Bede Empowers the Educator

Bede Works With You

Set the subjects, texts, and boundaries yourself in Parent Setup. Preview every response in a private "Ask Bede" sandbox, before your child ever sees it.

Bede Teaches Through Questions

Bede asks patient questions at a steady pace, built for the Grammar and Logic stages of childhood. After a round or two, it offers a hint or moves on.

Fits Real Families

Set up to ten students in one pod, each with a grade level, subjects, and daily plan. Teach at the kitchen table or run a small cooperative, either way.

Start Small if You Want To

New to homeschooling, or cautious about AI? Begin as a Book Companion — Bede simply discusses the book you are already reading, with a light touch and few interruptions. Add more structure when, and only when, you want it.

Pick Up Where You Left Off

Note where an interrupted lesson actually stopped and Bede opens mid-thread the next day, instead of interviewing your child about what they remember. Your note always outranks Bede's own.

Adapts to How Your Child Learns

Bede notices whether a learner does better writing, drawing, seeing, or talking it through, and shifts how it teaches. Where there is an honest signal to show, the Progress page tells you whether that is actually changing anything.

Adaptive Study, Real Evidence

Diagnostics That Earn Their Keep

Bede does not guess at what your child knows, and it does not quiz them to find out. It watches the ordinary work of the lesson — a narration given, a math problem reasoned through, a word sounded out — and quietly builds a calibrated picture of where mastery actually sits, so the next question lands in the right place.

Mathematics

Skill-by-skill mastery estimates from how your child reasons during the lesson. When math is part of the day, the session summary reports the real movement, before and after — measured, not guessed.

Composition

Narrations are scored against a rubric and rolled up over time, so growth in how a child tells back what they have read becomes visible instead of anecdotal.

Reading Foundations (K–2)

One brief, playful phonics check woven into an early Language Arts session — never a drill, never announced — following a real scope and sequence from sounds through blending to sight words.

Language Exposure

A Latin phrase for Rome, an Italian term for a sonata, a saint's own words in their own tongue. One at a time, in the lesson where it belongs, and Bede checks later whether it stuck.

Responsible AI at Home

The Privacy Barriers, Stated Plainly

It Runs on Your Network

Install Bede on a computer in your own house — Windows, Mac, Linux, even a Raspberry Pi. Children connect from their own tablets over your LAN. Running a lesson never requires a call back to us.

Encrypted Where It Sits

Voice profiles, student plans, transcripts, and assessments are AES-256-GCM encrypted in your database. The database provider never sees plaintext.

You Can Run It Fully Offline

Point Bede at a language model running on your own machine and no lesson text leaves the house at all. Prefer a cloud model instead? Switch providers from the parent screen, mid-day, without touching a config file.

Guarded in Real Time

Every message a child sends is checked before it reaches the tutor. Distress routes to a safeguarding response, not a lesson. Clear attempts to override Bede's rules or pull data out of it are redirected on the spot; subtler probing is logged, and a sustained pattern emails you.

A Digital System of Record

Narration history, mastery snapshots, session summaries, and an encrypted audit log of every security event — a real record for a real school year, kept where you can see it.

Deletion That Means Deletion

One type-to-confirm button removes everything belonging to a student — transcripts, narrations, mastery, voice profile, the lot. Not archived. Gone.

Responsible adoption is the point, not a feature. Bede's persona, ethics, and hard limits live in a written constitution that is checked for tampering every single time the software starts — if it has been altered, Bede refuses to run at all. No parent setting, no clever prompt from a child, and no update from us can quietly override it.

Sessions stop on their own. Breaks are mandatory every hour. Bede is a tutor and nothing more — never a priest, a therapist, or a parent — and it will not pretend to remember a conversation it never had. Teaching children to adopt this well means showing them a tool with edges, so Bede has edges.
