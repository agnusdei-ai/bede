School and charter FAQs

How do we request pricing for our campuses?

Email info@agnusdei.ai with your school name, campus count, learner estimates, and setup needs. Each additional campus needs its own license. Simultaneous use depends on tested hardware capacity.

Can we change the way we are billed during the year?

Yes, through a mutually agreed written amendment with an effective date. The 12-month commitment remains unless changed in writing. Per-learner and campus plans are not billed for the same campus period.

Is the equipment included in the software price?

No. Hardware, taxes, school-system integrations, onsite implementation and customization are priced separately. We confirm capacity on certified hardware before deployment.

How does Bede support teachers?

The current pilot supports synthetic assessment review, Data Day evidence, teacher-approved plans, and progress review. Teachers deliver instruction and choose next steps. Direct student tutoring requires separate qualification.

What is required before a school evaluation?

A school evaluation requires its own signed scope. Real learner data and direct tutoring require separate institutional, security, and deployment qualification. No customer agreement, school endorsement, or approved deployment is implied. Broader deployment requires a signed agreement; there is no automatic conversion.

Can Bede administer DIBELS or grade a student's character?

No. Educators may enter independently administered DIBELS results, but Bede does not administer or score the assessment. Faith and character are never scored.

Where is student information stored?

In a self-hosted deployment, student data stays on school equipment under the agreed security setup. Review disclosures, backups, access controls, and device/network requirements before signing.

What must a public charter school review?

Verify a nondevotional academic scope and review content, governance, privacy, and deployment. Disabling optional faith subjects alone does not establish suitability. No charter approval or compliance certification is implied.
