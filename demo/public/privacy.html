Privacy Notice — Public Beta Demo

Applies only to the free public demo at this domain. Last updated 2026-08-03.

This page covers the public beta demo only — the version anyone can try with a one-time 6-digit code, no account required. It's a completely different deployment from Bede itself, which real families run entirely on their own hardware with nothing sent to us at all. If you're evaluating Bede for your own family's use, the self-hosted product's privacy model is stricter than everything below — see the main project's documentation for that.

In short: we never store the conversation itself. We do store, briefly, the name/grade you optionally provide and anonymized structural signals about how the session went (which tools were used, how many turns, which subjects) — never what was actually said. If you use the microphone, your audio is sent to OpenAI to be transcribed into text for that turn only, and is not stored by us or by them. Feedback is entirely optional, and we never ask a child for their own email address.

Information we collect from your child

This demo does not use accounts — there is no username, password, phone number, or payment information collected from your child at any point. What we collect, only with your consent, is limited to:

  • Name and grade level (optional) — used only to personalize the one session in progress, kept for at most 6 hours, and never built into a persistent profile.
  • A short note on what you're already learning at home (optional) — e.g. "reading Farmer Boy together" — used only so Bede can weave that into its questions during the one session in progress ("Continuing Mastery"), kept for the same at-most-6-hours window as name and grade above, and never built into a persistent profile.
  • Your family's church tradition (optional) — e.g. "Baptist", "Catholic", "Non-denominational". The demo shows every subject to every visitor, including both Scripture & Bible Study and Saints & Catechism side by side, so this label is used only so Bede frames that content consistently with your own tradition instead of assuming one. Kept for the same at-most-6-hours window as the fields above, then deleted automatically, and never built into a persistent profile.
    This one is different from the others, and we want to be plain about it: a church tradition says something about your family's religious beliefs, which privacy law treats as a special category deserving extra care. It is entirely optional, the demo works exactly the same if you leave it blank, and we would rather you skipped it than felt any pressure to fill it in. If you do provide it, it is used for this one session's framing and nothing else — never to categorize, segment, target, or profile your family, and never shared with anyone.
  • A record of the work finished during the session — which skill was worked, how much help it took, and what Bede noticed about the work itself (for example that a narration was one to show, or that a step went further than what was asked). This is shown back to you on screen during the session so you can see what your learner actually did. It never includes anything that was said, it is kept for the same at-most-6-hours window as the fields above, and it is deleted immediately if you log out. It is never built into a persistent profile, and it is never a grade, a score, or a judgment about your child.
  • The conversation itself, transmitted in real time to our AI providers, OpenAI (primary) and, only if OpenAI is briefly unavailable, our configured backup — Mistral by default, or Claude (Anthropic) if we've configured that as the backup instead — so Bede can generate its tutoring responses. Never stored by us and never used by any of these providers to train their models, per their own terms.
  • Your voice, if you use the microphone (optional) — the audio passes through our server to OpenAI's transcription service to be turned into text, and is not stored beyond that one turn by us or by OpenAI. Bede's own spoken reply is generated as audio by OpenAI's text-to-speech service.
  • Anonymized interaction-pattern signals (which teaching tools were used, how many turns, which subjects) — cryptographically keyed so it cannot be traced back to your child, and never includes anything that was actually said.

We do not require your child to provide more information than is reasonably necessary to try the demo, and every field above is optional except the conversation itself, which is inherent to how a tutoring session works and is not retained afterward.

Information we collect only from a parent or guardian

At the end of a session, we show an optional "how could this be better?" prompt. A child is never asked for their own contact information. If a follow-up email address is provided, our interface requires you to first affirm that you are the parent or guardian before that field even appears, and the address is used once, to reply, and is never written to a database.

How we use this information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Personalize the single tutoring session in progress.
  • Generate Bede's tutoring responses and spoken voice (via OpenAI, with a configured backup — Mistral by default, or Claude/Anthropic instead — for text responses only, if OpenAI is briefly unavailable).
  • Transcribe your microphone audio into text, via OpenAI, so Bede can respond to it.
  • Understand, in aggregate and anonymized form, which teaching patterns work well, so we can improve Bede.
  • Reply to a parent or guardian who has asked us to.

Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell your child's information, and we do not use it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers that make the demo work — OpenAI (to generate Bede's responses and synthesize its spoken voice), our configured backup text provider (Mistral by default, or Claude/Anthropic if we've configured that instead, used only if OpenAI is briefly unavailable), and Resend (to deliver an optional feedback email) — each of whom is contractually and technically limited to using it for that purpose. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Your parental rights under COPPA

At any time, you may:

  • Ask what personal information we hold related to your child's session.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Withdraw your consent and end your child's session.

In practice there is very little to review or delete once a session ends — name/grade expire automatically within 6 hours and the conversation itself is never stored — but if you'd like to exercise these rights, or believe your child shared more than described here, contact us at info@agnusdei.ai with your child's session details and we'll act on it promptly.

What happens if you do not consent

If you do not agree at the consent step, your child will not be able to use this demo. Nothing else on this page requires consent beyond that single gate — there is no further, separate collection hidden inside the session itself.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, usernames, or passwords — there is nothing to create or remember.
  • No phone numbers and no payment information are ever collected in this demo.
  • No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics pixels, no ad networks.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • No persistent account or profile is created from a demo session — a session code is discarded (or expires) and that's the end of it.
