# Program-aware Shakespeare support

## Evidence and actual delivery

The owner clarified that Bede tutors alongside the learner's school or
publisher curriculum. Shared authors and a broadly classical method do not,
by themselves, establish a faithful program mirror.

This increment therefore keeps **program placement**, **assigned edition**,
**Bede's verified passage**, and **Bede's support sequence** distinct. It adds
first-party factual course matches to the existing twelve-unit supplement.
It does not complete or replace a publisher's full curriculum or duplicate
the separate Grade 8 and Grades 9–12 year-authoring work.

The previous Midsummer floor of 6 and Caesar floor of 8 excluded the actual
listed school placements. Their Bede floors are now 5 and 6. Each of their
three weeks has a concrete bilingual guided entry route, with chunking,
vocabulary, narration, optional listening and source-grounded questions.
Placement is not a readiness diagnosis; adult preview and support choice
remain required. All other unit floors and the accepted Grade 8 year remain
unchanged.

Caesar's final transfer exercise stays with Antony and an original neutral
announcement, with North as the actual supplied background. It does not
introduce the later-grade Merchant passage into the Grade 6 route merely
because that passage is available elsewhere in Bede.

[Valor's IHP description](https://www.valoreducation.org/integrated-humanities-program)
applies to Grades 9–12. The tutor uses original Bede close-reading, seminar
and panel-preparation prompts for that range. Lower-school preparation is
kept separate: no Grade 5 or 6 activity is represented as an IHP panel or
school assessment. Neither a panel nor the annual symposium is marked
complete by a rehearsal.

Explicit noncoverage within this supplement: The Comedy of Errors (7),
Richard II (11), Hamlet, King Lear and Othello (12). The published list also
contains younger illustrated adaptations; the original Bede extracts are
not presented as those editions. Arts and music remain available under
Bede's own sequence, without inventing a Valor artist/composer rotation.

## Selection contract

- Both demo and enrolled parent setups select Bede, Valor, Memoria Press or
  another adult-selected curriculum. The catalog and direct lesson endpoint
  enforce the selected program's actual verified coverage and grade floor.
- Earlier listed works remain reviewable by older learners; their original
  listed placement remains visible. The interface does not silently relabel
  an earlier work as the current year's school assignment.
- Weeks 1–3 are explicitly **Bede support weeks**. The adult retains the
  school's or publisher's actual calendar and assigned reading.
- The selected lesson displays its exact supplied passage and the assigned-
  edition distinction. Historical text pagination and modern editorial
  notes are not treated as interchangeable.
- Before applying a non-Bede selection, the adult checks the reading,
  edition and supporting passage. Its public selection token binds the
  confirmation to the pinned release, program, unit, grade, support week
  and support style. It is not authentication, a secret or proof that a
  school approved the curriculum.
- The approved English and Spanish variants belong to the same pinned
  release. A language change preserves the assignment; a changed source
  release or relevant selection requires another review.
- The existing editable unit-note field persists the selection through the
  normal parent save. Selecting a program or previewing a lesson never
  saves automatically. Legacy Bede unit notes continue to work unchanged.
- Other curricula use adult-checked Bede passages without invented
  publisher placements or answers. No affiliation or endorsement is claimed.

The public review packet includes this distinction in both languages and
the actual program-specific preparation prompts. Private adult models stay
outside the public repository and API. Tutoring remains disabled by default
until a coordinated release decision; this review increment does not change
production flags, accounts, databases or deployments.
