# Thirty-six-week scope and sequence

## How to use the map

**Weeks 1–9 have authored teaching materials in document 04. Weeks 10–36 are planned units requiring lesson preparation.** Adult teaching, safety and Latin review gates still apply. Each course entry gives objective → reading/input → assignment → assessment. Material codes identify the reusable kit; specific extra materials appear in the biology ledger.

- **L:** core literary edition from document 02, notebook, annotation tools.
- **H:** USG/UNG *World History: Cultures, States, and Societies to 1500*, exact H01–H36 selections in the [replacement register](resources/REPLACEMENT-REGISTER.md), blank learner-drawn world map/timeline, source log.
- **M:** Wallace *Beginning and Intermediate Algebra*, exact M01–M36 sections/original inputs in the replacement register, graph paper/ruler, calculator for checking only after reasoning.
- **B:** [original biology reference notes](resources/biology-reference-notes.md), exact B01–B36, notebook, lab kit/ledger; no external figures.
- **T:** D'Ooge grammar topic, vocabulary cards, original/adapted sentences.
- **C:** included studio guide, shared computer/Python or paper cards, public/non-sensitive data.
- **P/A:** PE/health and practical-arts weekly strands at the end of this map.

For H/B/M the register's matching week row is the exact reading assignment, including review weeks; there are no inherited chapter aliases. Apply document 02's paragraph/example/time caps and asset exclusions. Q1 uses document 04's original exercises; later math practice uses the bounded odd-item bank in document 02 plus original explanation tasks, with teacher daily checks. Latin spirals prior forms each week. “Check” means an adult looks at the work and asks a diagnostic question, not automatic scoring.
