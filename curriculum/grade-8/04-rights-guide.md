# Source and rights register guide

## Status meaning

- **APPROVED CANDIDATE:** this research found a plausible rights-compatible bounded item. **Still requires educational suitability, exact-file verification, authenticated rights review and ingestion approval.** It is not an approved course source.
- **EXTERNAL ONLY:** a link/reference for a human to consult under its terms. No copying into Bede delivery/corpus, provider processing or training authorized.
- **HOLD:** missing/conflicting rights, version, asset, jurisdiction or suitability evidence. No activation.
- **REJECT:** unsuitable for the stated intended use. A different use needs a separately reviewed record; do not silently change rationale.

Initial set is bounded to12 entries spanning the requested areas, not an adopted reading list, complete rights clearance or content corpus. No candidate file has been downloaded for ingestion or training.

## Priority and proprietary boundaries

Prefer verified US public-domain/CC0 exact assets and independently authored Bede instruction; next consider CC BY and then separable CC BY-SA materials with feasible obligations. Public domain in the USA is not a worldwide clearance. Ancient authors do not make a modern translation, recording, photograph or editorial apparatus public domain.

Bede-owned new curriculum/code/tooling remains proprietary. “Original” is not “public domain.” No automatic open license is applied here. Existing separately licensed components keep their own notices. A CC BY-SA adapted reading must satisfy ShareAlike for that adaptation; it does **not automatically require opening an unrelated application**, but legal boundaries must be reviewed before mixing/embedding. If separability/obligations are unclear, hold or external-only. Noncommercial, no-derivatives, proprietary or unclear sources are not commercially ingested by assumption.

Do not copy another curriculum's selection/sequence structure or teaching notes. A public-domain underlying book does not automatically clear a modern curriculum's curated annotation, worksheet or compilation. No AI paraphrasing or translation to evade rights.

## Seven separate use judgments

Every entry records:

1. **Copy/distribute:** excerpts/full text/assets; commercial resale; attribution/notices.
2. **Adapt:** abridgment, translation, exercises and derivative works.
3. **TTS:** distinguish rights to render text from ownership/redistribution/provider terms of a recording/voice; preserve accessible local options.
4. **Store/offline:** exact file/edition, notices, asset exceptions, retention and jurisdiction.
5. **Index/retrieve:** indexing, excerpt reproduction and the **current code gate** are separate from legal permission.
6. **Model-provider processing:** requires rights plus provider contract, privacy, retention, data location and permitted-purpose review; never assume upload permission from free reading access.
7. **Train:** **NOT AUTHORIZED BY THIS PROJECT** for all initial entries, even where an underlying license may legally permit it. Do not imply research has authorized training.

Values “potentially permitted by stated license” are conditional legal research, **not an activation decision**. Authenticated rights review can narrow permissions further.

## Current software gate is intentionally stricter

At the inspected source snapshot, verbatim ContentChunk requires `public_domain_verified`; licensed content is not equivalent. Local retrieval excludes general `licensed` records pending a qualified license-scope contract. This register must not bypass either gate or turn CC0/CC BY metadata into an authenticated database approval.

Any future treatment of CC0 as verified-PD needs legal/edition review and an authorized record. CC BY Spanish, PSF Python documentation and BY-SA planning materials are not relabeled public domain. Future factory specification must express rights compatibility and fail closed, with review/revocation/version binding.

## Evidence and editions

Each JSON entry supplies source ID, author/editor/translator, version, publisher and exact page; subject/use; license evidence URL/date/jurisdiction; exceptions/attribution; per-use permissions; accessibility/demand/currency/culture; equipment/cost; reviewer; status/rationale.

For live HTML, “checked2026-11-03, file not pinned” means exact **page** verified, not exact **bytes** pinned. Before ingestion record the downloaded asset's permitted acquisition, hash, revision, relevant rights statement and notices; no such acquisition is authorized here.

Accessibility notes are desk review, not assistive-technology testing. Research reviewer is the assistant collecting evidence, not an authenticated human legal/academic approver. `human_review` remains pending on every item.

## Important findings

- Gutenberg Alice/Douglass catalogs mark the specific text editions public domain in the USA. Respect redistribution/trademark conditions; exclude unverified illustrations/modern introductions/audio.
- Natural Earth110m land page displays version4.0.0; policy declares map data public domain. Use physical land data, not a claim that disputed borders are neutral or current.
- Smithsonian's specific Mary Vaux Walcott image and metadata have asset-specific CC0 markings. Other museum objects are not cleared by association.
- Semillas publisher states CC BY4.0 for the book **except where otherwise noted**. Review each chosen text/media item; modern language is a candidate, not Grade 8 selection.
- Current inspected OpenStax Prealgebra2e preface states BY-NC-SA4.0. Old “CC BY” PDFs/catalogs cannot clear the current edition.
- Illustrative Mathematics rights indications vary across site/edition and current derivatives. The task page alone did not expose a definitive asset-specific license in extracted text; conflicting NC/BY signals mean **HOLD**, not guessed commercial permission.
- NOAA/CDC agency reuse policies explicitly allow exceptions. A general government label is not asset-level proof; initial entries are held/external-only until selected text and images are checked.
- Historic Faraday experiments are not a safe current Grade 8 practical manual.
- A source being useful evidence in this memo does not authorize copying its curriculum into Bede.
