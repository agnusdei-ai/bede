For the people who run co-ops

Bede is a classical, Socratic tutor that a homeschooling family runs on their own computer. It introduces a lesson, asks the child to narrate it back, and sends them to the real book. Your families meet with you once or twice a week. This is about the other three or four days.

We have built and tested it in one family. We do not know what a co-op would need, who decides, or whether you would want it at all. Six minutes, and you can leave your name off it. If you would rather just talk, write to info@agnusdei.ai and a person will answer.

Never seen it? Try the demo first, or read the FAQ. Neither is required to answer this.

Does the co-op set curriculum, or does each family choose? Bede is built to defer to the parent as the child's primary educator. A co-op that sets curriculum for its members is a case we have never designed against, and we would like to know how common it is.
Does your co-op hold a statement of faith or a required tradition? Bede ships a Catholic Saints & Catechism module and a separate, deliberately denomination-neutral Scripture module beside it. A family enables either, both, or neither.
