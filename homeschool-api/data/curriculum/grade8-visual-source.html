Grade 8 original visual source and reading aids

Bede-original clothed-pose schematic, made from geometric shapes. It is not Michelangelo’s Libyan Sibyl, a tracing, a photograph or a substitute for evidence that the omitted artwork was independently studied. Original Bede instruction remains proprietary.

Text-access description

The head is above a tilted covered torso. One covered arm bends outward and the other is lower. Two covered leg shapes end in broad flat feet above a horizontal baseline. Compare the torso edge, arm directions, negative spaces and the vertical dashed guide. This is a schematic visual source, not a scientifically measured body or a model answer about an artist’s intention.

Using the alternative

Use the original figure to study formal grouping, axes and a supported interpretation when the external figure study is unsuitable or unavailable. Keep analysis of this source distinct from unperformed observation of the canonical artwork. A tactile/verbal reconstruction can support spatial reasoning; it does not certify an unperformed sensory task.

Reading-boundary marker

North’s assigned ranges use the1909 edition’s printed pages. Assign a paragraph to the range containing its first word. Read every paragraph starting in that range and finish it in full; skip paragraphs starting earlier. A paragraph starting on18 and ending on19 belongs only to the1–18 assignment, not19–36. Mark the last paragraph read locally; do not omit the rest of a page. Record edition conversions rather than inventing a page equivalence.
