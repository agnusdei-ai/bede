1 | Purpose and permitted use
Provider will process only information specifically authorized by School to deliver documented educational services,
under School direction and the signed scope. Provider will not use protected student information for targeted
advertising, sell or rent student information, build unrelated commercial profiles, or use identifiable student records
to train models unless a separate, lawful written approval explicitly permits it.

2 | Disclosure and access
School decides whether a lawful FERPA basis permits disclosure and remains responsible for school-level permissions
and notices. If the FERPA school-official exception applies, Provider performs a School-designated institutional
service, acts under School direct control for education-record use, restricts staff access by legitimate educational need,
and must not re-disclose data except as authorized by the agreement and law.

3 | COPPA, Texas consent and notice
Before use involving children under 13, the parties will determine whether COPPA applies and document the proper
parental or school authorization, notices and school-purpose limits. The ability of a school to consent under COPPA is
limited to school-benefit uses; it is not a blanket substitute for state-mandated direct and informed parental consent.
School will verify Texas Education Code Section 32.1021 and its adopted policies before activating a school application
for pupils.

4 | Security safeguards
Before any real-data launch, Provider must present the agreed security controls and evidence: role and campus
separation; strong staff authentication; encryption in transit and at rest; secure secrets handling; monitoring; tested
recovery; least-privilege support; vulnerability response; and verified revocation. Unverified controls do not become
contractual facts by being listed here. School may decline real-data access until testing satisfies the agreed standard.

5 | Subprocessors and model services
No third-party model or ancillary service receives identifiable School student information unless named in an
approved Exhibit B describing company, purpose, data fields, geography, retention, and governing terms. Changes to
subprocessors require the agreed notice and objection process. The school-approved deployment profile must
explicitly indicate local model versus cloud adapter and audio processing paths.

6 | Retention, return, deletion and backups
Provider will collect the minimum data needed, honor School-approved retention periods, support access/correction
requests within agreed roles, and return or delete covered information upon authorized School request or
termination. Texas Education Code Section 32.156 specifies deletion within 60 days after a district request unless a
contract/terms provision or permitted student/parent consent changes the rule. A schedule for active data, derived
records, logs, archived backups and recovery copies must be completed before real data is used; do not promise
immediate deletion from immutable backups without a verified method.

7 | Security events, audit and termination
Provider will isolate and investigate suspected unauthorized access, preserve relevant evidence, notify School using
contacts and deadlines filled in Schedule C, and cooperate with legally required notifications. The parties will specify
incident severity, notification window, investigation responsibilities, subcontractor cooperation, indemnity/insurance
expectations, governing law, dispute process and order of precedence in the executed agreement.
